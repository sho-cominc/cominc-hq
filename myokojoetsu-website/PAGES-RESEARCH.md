### SEE — Detailed Research

#### Takada Castle (高田城)
- Built 1614 by Matsudaira Tadateru (6th son of Tokugawa Ieyasu), overseen by Date Masamune
- Tenka Bushin project: 13+ feudal families conscripted to build it in just 4 months
- **No stone walls** — entirely earthwork fortifications + moat system (unusual for its political importance)
- **Cherry blossoms**: ~4,000 Somei Yoshino trees, ~3,000 bonbori lanterns, reflected in moat. Top 3 night sakura in Japan (with Hirosaki & Yoshino). Festival dates: late March–mid April, illumination 18:00–21:00. 100th anniversary celebrated 2025
- **Lotus**: 19-hectare outer moat, one of the world's largest lotus ponds. Dates to 1871 when Hosaka Sadakichi planted lotus commercially after domain's financial collapse. Recognized by paleobotanist Oga Ichiro ("Dr. Lotus") in 1953. Festival mid-July–mid-August
- **History Museum**: 620 yen adults, 320 yen students. Combined with Sanju Yagura turret

#### Mt. Myoko (妙高山, 2,454m)
- Sacred since 708 AD (Nara period). Sekiyama Shrine (関山神社) at base
- Center for Shugendo mountain asceticism. Annual pilgrimages of up to 1,200 people
- Also called "Echigo Fuji" (越後富士) for its symmetry
- **Hiking**: Most popular = Tsubame Trail from Tsubame Onsen. Splits into two paths (Kita-jigokudani Valley vs Sotaki Falls route, 80m drop). 8-9 hours round trip. Strenuous
- Summit: 360° panorama — Northern Alps to Sea of Japan, Sado Island on clear days
- Part of Myoko-Togakushi Renzan National Park (established 2015)

#### Mt. Hiuchi (火打山, 2,462m)
- Highest peak in Niigata Prefecture. 100 Famous Mountains + 100 Famous Flower Mountains
- **Tengu-no-niwa** (天狗の庭): High-altitude marshland. Featured on CNN "31 Most Beautiful Places in Japan"
- Alpine flowers: Hakusan kozakura, Myoko torikabuto, Japanese cotton grass
- Standard route from Sasagamine → Kurosawaike Pond → Mt. Chausu → Koyaike Pond → summit
- Can link to Mt. Myoko as 2-day traverse with mountain hut overnight

#### Naena Falls (苗名滝)
- 55m waterfall on Sekigawa River. One of Japan's 100 Famous Waterfalls
- Nickname: "Earthquake Waterfall" (地震滝) for the thundering impact
- Framed by steep basalt walls. Best: spring (snowmelt power), autumn (foliage over basalt)
- Easy 15-minute flat walk from car park

#### Imori Pond (いもり池)
- 500m circumference at foot of Mt. Myoko (Ikenotaira Onsen area)
- Perfect mirror reflection of Mt. Myoko on windless days
- **Mizubasho**: 100,000+ wild Asian skunk cabbage, mid-April to early May
- Boardwalk circles the pond. Iconic autumn foliage reflection spot

#### Gangi (雁木) Covered Arcades
- **16 km total** — longest covered arcade system in Japan. Uniform 1.80m width
- Originating early Edo period. Snow Country engineering: keeps walkways passable under heavy snow
- Takada's system uniquely intact (Nagaoka, Sanjo, Itoigawa, Kuroishi retain only fragments)
- Notable stops: former Imai Dyeing Shop, former Kanazu Cooper Shop, Takada Komachi Machiya Exchange Hall

#### Kasugayama Castle (春日山城跡)
- Fortress of Uesugi Kenshin — one of Japan's Top 100 Castles, National Historic Site since 1935
- Among Japan's Five Greatest Mountain Castles
- Kenshin became lord 1548. Base for 1561 Battle of Kawanakajima vs Takeda Shingen
- No stone walls — earthen ramparts, dry moats, terraced enclosures on 180m summit
- 30-min walk to summit passes Bishamondo (Kenshin's prayer spot). Panoramic views from Honmaru
- Story Museum at base with Kawanakajima battle screens

#### Naoetsu (直江津)
- Important Sea of Japan port since Kamakura period (1192–1333)
- **Shinran connection**: Buddhist monk exiled from Kyoto in 1207, landed at Kotagahama beach. Spent ~4 years here
- **Gion Festival**: 530+ years tradition, late July (26–29). Mikoshi processions, float parades, taiko, fireworks over Seki River

### STAY — Detailed Research

#### Myoko's Seven Hot Springs (妙高七湯)

| Onsen | Water | Character |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Akakura (赤倉)** | Clear, calcium sulfate + bicarbonate | "Beautiful skin water" — dual spring, rare |
| **Shin-Akakura (新赤倉)** | Clear, sulfate | Gentler version of Akakura |
| **Ikenotaira (池の平)** | Clear and black | Black water soothes dry skin, eczema |
| **Myoko (妙高)** | Clear, sulfate | Blood circulation, wound healing |
| **Suginosawa (杉野沢)** | Clear, sulfate | Mineral-rich, calming |
| **Tsubame (燕)** | Milky white, sulfur + bicarbonate | High altitude, said to prevent high blood pressure |
| **Seki (関)** | Reddish-brown, high iron | Strong heating effect, oldest Myoko onsen (est. 1729) |

#### Notable Properties
- **Akakura Kanko Hotel (赤倉観光ホテル)**: Founded 1937 by Okura Zaibatsu. White Alpine-style at 1,000m. Views to Nojiri Lake + Sado Island. Ski-in/ski-out. SPA & SUITE wing 2009, Premium wing 2016. Myoko's most distinguished address
- **Tsubame Onsen wild baths**: Two free outdoor baths at Mt. Myoko trailhead — Ougon-no-yu (黄金の湯, gender-separated) and Kawara-no-yu (河原の湯, mixed, cliff-side next to river). Milky white. Open ~June–December
- **Seki Onsen**: Oldest of the seven (1729). Rust-red iron water. Heating effect lingers long after bathing

#### Mountain Lodges
- **A.I.R. Myoko**: Boutique, ski-in/ski-out on Akakura slopes. Japanese cedar furniture, contemporary pottery, western beds. Plant-forward cuisine. Private onsen
- **Koya-ike Hutte (高谷池ヒュッテ)**: Between Myoko & Hiuchi. 100 guests. ¥6,000/night. Tent camping ¥900
- **Kurosawa-ike Hutte (黒沢池ヒュッテ)**: Distinctive blue dome-shaped hut. Reservation-based. 4hrs to Mt. Myoko summit

#### Kominka Stays
- **MAHORA Nishinoya (まほら西の谷)**: 120-year farmhouse in Myoko City. 15 min from Joetsu-Myoko Station. Irori living room, engawa veranda, former cow barn → kitchen/library. Exclusive group rental

#### Modern Hotels
- **Lotte Arai Resort**: Japan's Best Ski Resort (2020 World Ski Awards). 1hr 46min from Tokyo. Full luxury: pool, hot spring, spa, two restaurants. Rates from ~$70/night off-season
- **Machiyado Oranchi**: 100-year traditional house, "history of Joetsu Myoko" theme. Japanese architecture + Scandinavian interior. Mt. Myoko views

### SEASONS — Detailed Research

#### WINTER (Dec–Mar)

**Ski Resorts**:
- **Suginohara (杉ノ原)**: Japan's longest single run — 8.5km, 1,124m vertical. Free kids skiing all season
- **Seki Onsen (関温泉)**: 2 lifts, no grooming. 1,000-year-old hot spring at base with reddish-brown mineral water. Powder ~70% of peak season. 14–16m annual snowfall. Cult favorite among powder hunters
- **Akakura Onsen + Kanko**: Kanko est. 1937, one of Japan's first European-style ski resorts. Shared lift pass. Rotenburo overlooking Madarao under falling snow
- **Lotte Arai**: Originally opened 1993 by Hideo Morita (son of Sony co-founder Akio Morita). Closed 2006, seized by city for unpaid taxes, bought by Lotte for ¥1.8B in 2016, reopened Dec 2017. 1,000+ hectares
- **Madarao (斑尾)**: 60% ungroomed, 15 tree-run courses — one of Japan's largest tree-skiing networks. 10–13m annual snowfall

**What "13 metres" means**: Myoko averages 13–15m cumulative snowfall per season. 2014-15 topped 16m. The 1944-45 season recorded **30.9 metres** at Sekiyama Station — among the highest ever measured on Earth. Mechanism: Sea of Japan moisture hits the Myoko mountain wall

**Sake brewing**: Niigata has ~89-91 breweries (most of any prefecture). **Joetsu City alone has 12 breweries** (broader region: 20). Snowmelt soft water from Myoko mountains + local sake rice (Gohyakumangoku, Koshi-Tanrei). **Shiboritate (搾りたて)** — freshly pressed unpasteurized new sake — arrives December, often only at brewery door

**Winter food**:
- Noppe-jiru (のっぺい汁): Root vegetable stew, soy-base, sesame oil. New Year staple
- Kanzuri process: Peppers salted in autumn → laid on January snow 3-4 days (yukisarashi) → mixed with koji + yuzu → fermented minimum 3 years (up to 6). Each barrel hand-turned once/year. Snow-bleaching draws out salt/bitterness via osmosis
- Sasadango: 500-year-old battlefield preserved food

**Editorial hook**: *In Myoko, you don't go to the snow — the snow comes to you, silently, relentlessly, 13 metres of it, and the only sound is the creak of timber under its weight.*

#### SPRING (Apr–May)

**Cherry blossoms**: Takada Castle — 4,000 trees, 3,000 bonbori lanterns. CNN named it one of world's 5 best flower festivals. Trees first planted 1909 by veterans' association (2,200 trees). Festival began 1926, 100th anniversary 2025. Peak: early–mid April. 300m Sakura Road with moat reflection is centerpiece

**Hoshitoge Rice Terraces (星峠の棚田)**: ~200 terraced paddies near Tokamachi (50 min from Joetsu IC). Late April–mid May: paddies become "mizu-kagami" (水鏡) water mirrors. At sunrise, mist rises creating sea of clouds beneath mirrored paddies

**Iwanohara Vineyard (岩の原葡萄園)**: Japan's oldest winery, founded 1890 by Zenbei Kawakami ("father of Japanese wine"). Crossbred **10,311 grape varieties** to develop 22 suited to snowy Niigata, including Muscat Bailey A — still one of Japan's signature grapes. No. 1 Ishikura stone cellar = registered Tangible Cultural Property. No. 2 cellar uses **yukimuro** (snow-based natural refrigerator)

**Naena Falls spring**: Snowmelt dramatically amplifies the 55m waterfall. Nickname "Earthquake Falls" from ancient word "nai"

**Editorial hook**: *Four thousand lanterns, four thousand cherry trees, and a castle moat that holds the reflection of both — Takada in April is Japan at its most unguarded.*

#### SUMMER (Jun–Aug)

**Mt. Hiuchi**: CNN listed Koya-ike Marsh among world's top 25 most beautiful spots. Tengu-no-niwa = northernmost ptarmigan (raichō) habitat in Japan. Alpine flowers peak mid-late July. 4.5hrs one way to summit

**Takada lotus**: 19ha moat. Started 1871 as commercial crop when domain went bankrupt. Recognized by Dr. Oga Ichiro 1953. Designated "most Oriental lotus" (東洋一の蓮). Festival mid-July–mid-August

**Naoetsu Gion Festival**: July 26-29. ~5,000 fireworks over Seki River mouth. Mikoshi processions. Rice barrel offerings on July 29. Naoetsu Beach: wide shallow sandy shore (rare on Sea of Japan coast), occasional dolphin sightings

**Gangi in summer**: 16km covered arcades create cool, shaded passages through old machiya townhouses. "Gangi no Machi Saisei" preservation groups maintain the network

**Editorial hook**: *A 19-hectare moat of lotus blooms that began as a bankrupt domain's last resort — Takada in summer is proof that necessity, left alone long enough, becomes beauty.*

#### AUTUMN (Sep–Nov)

**Foliage progression**: Sasagamine begins first week of October, lower elevations follow through the month. 6-week window by adjusting altitude. Imori Pond peak: mid-late October. Naena Falls: late October–mid November

**Rice harvest**: Niigata = Japan's top rice prefecture. **Koshihikari cultivar developed in Niigata in 1956** — bred for extreme day/night temperature variation, snowmelt water, alluvial soil. Shinmai (新米, new rice) from late September: higher moisture, pronounced sweetness, plump stickiness

**Hiyaoroshi sake**: Brewed in spring, pasteurized once, matured through summer. Rounder, deeper, more settled than winter's shiboritate. Aki-agari (秋上がり) = sake that specifically improved through summer aging. **12 breweries in Joetsu City** for autumn tasting

**Kanburi**: Cold yellowtail migration begins off Joetsu coast in late autumn. Peak fat content. Buri festivals, best served at Naoetsu waterfront izakaya

**Editorial hook**: *In October, the mountain is on fire and the rice is in — and in Joetsu, the only appropriate response to both is a glass of hiyaoroshi at room temperature.*

### SHOP — Detailed Research

#### Sake — Joetsu: Japan's Quiet Sake Capital
- Niigata: ~89 breweries (most of any prefecture). **Joetsu City alone: 12 breweries. Broader region: 20**
- **Water**: Myoko snowmelt, soft, filtered through granite. Iron/calcium-free
- **Rice**: Yoshikawa district = largest growing area for **Gohyakumangoku (五百万石)**, developed at Niigata agricultural station 1938. Drives the **tanrei karakuchi (淡麗辛口)** style — clean, crisp, light, dry
- **Brewers**: Echigo Toji (越後杜氏) guild = one of Japan's Three Great Brewer's Guilds

**Notable Breweries**:

| Brewery | Brand | Founded | Highlights |
|---------|-------|---------|------------|
| **Takeda Shuzo (竹田酒造店)** | Katafune (潟舟) | 1866 | IWC Trophy winner (2013, 2015). Unusually sweet/round for Niigata. 9th generation. Naoetsu area |
| **Maruyama Shuzojo (丸山酒造場)** | Setchubai (雪中梅) | 1822 | Myoko snowmelt water. Box koji method. Umeshu won gold at France's Feminaries 2022. NO visits |
| **Musashino Shuzo (武蔵野酒造)** | Ski-Masamune (スキー正宗) | 1916 | Toji Kenji Fujii: 40+ years master brewer. **Open year-round for tours/tastings** |
| **Kiminoi Shuzo (君の井酒造)** | Kiminoi (君の井) | 1842 | "Emperor's Well" — Meiji Emperor liked their sake. Yamahai method. Multiple golds. Arai, Myoko |
| **Joetsu Shuzo (上越酒造)** | Echigo Bijin (越後美人) | 1804 | 200+ years. Tank squeezing (槽搾り) preserved after 2021 modernization |
| **Myoko Shuzo (妙高酒造)** | Myokosan (妙高山) | 200+ yrs | Myoko City |

**Where to taste**: Echigo Kenshin SAKE Festival (October, Takada Honmachi arcade, 30+ booths, ¥3,000 day pass, 40,000 visitors). Sakura Plaza at Joetsu-Myoko Station

#### Kanzuri (かんずり) — Snow-Fermented Chili Paste
- Made by **single family-run company** in Myoko City. Name is trademarked (like Champagne)
- **4 ingredients only**: Togarashi chili, yuzu, koji, salt
- Process: Summer harvest → autumn salt-marination → **January yuki-sarashi** (spread on snow 3-4 days, osmosis draws out bitterness) → mix with koji + yuzu → ferment minimum 3 years (up to 6)
- **Kanzuri Factory**: 15 min walk from Arai Station. Tours, free tasting, shop. Best visit: mid-January–February during yuki-sarashi
- Local price: 600-800 yen for 4oz jar

#### Traditional Crafts

**Gangi covered arcades**: 16km total, longest in Japan. Honmachi arcade: 750m with ~200 specialty shops. Hosts annual SAKE Festival

**Niigata blades (connection to Joetsu)**:
- **Echigo Yoita Uchihamono**: Designated Traditional Craft. Origin 1578 — blacksmiths brought from Kasugayama (Joetsu!) by Uesugi vassal Naoe Sanetsuna. Planes, chisels, axes, adzes + kitchen knives
- **Echigo Sanjo Uchihamono**: Designated Traditional Craft. Dates to 1625

**UNESCO textiles**:
- **Echigo Jofu + Ojiya Chijimi**: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (2009). Ramie fiber, hand-split by fingernail. Final step: **yukizarashi** — wet fabric on snow 10-20 days, ozone from evaporation naturally bleaches. Ojiya Chijimi's crinkled texture (shibo) = one of Japan's most prized summer fabrics

#### Food Products Worth Taking Home
- **Koshihikari rice**: Uonuma Koshihikari has "A" taste ranking for 28 consecutive years. Terrace-grown Joetsu Koshihikari from Nishikidori is premium
- **Sasa dango**: ~500 years old, possibly Uesugi Kenshin's battlefield food. Kotake Seika (Joetsu, est. 1924) makes unique Sasa-Dango Pan bread
- **Myoko Kogen Beer**: Local craft brewery
- **Setchubai products**: Cheesecake, jelly, cake made with doburoku from Maruyama brewery
- **Straw animals (わら細工)**: Handmade by local artisans during winter. Available at Michi-no-Eki roadside stations

### EAT — Detailed Research

#### Hegi Soba (へぎそば)
- Niigata's signature noodle. Made with **funori (布海苔) seaweed** instead of wheat flour — gives silky, elastic texture with firm bite
- "Hegi" = rectangular wooden tray. Noodles arranged in elegant bite-sized bundles
- **Textile connection**: Funori was originally used in weaving Ojiya Chijimi textiles — weavers discovered it made extraordinary noodles. Food culture born from textile culture

#### Seafood — Japan Sea
- **Nodoguro (のどぐろ / Akamutsu)**: "Wagyu of the Sea" — rich, fatty deep-sea fish. Peaks autumn/winter
- **Kanburi (寒ブリ)**: Cold yellowtail, December, peak fat content
- **Gunchan (ぐんちゃん)**: Most popular seafood restaurant near Naoetsu
- **Tomi Zushi (富寿司)** Naoetsu branch: top sushi
- **Marine Dream Nou (マリンドリーム能生)**: Itoigawa roadside station with Japan's largest direct-from-boat red snow crab market (Kaniya Yokocho). Boiled crab Mar–Dec, self-grill shellfish

#### Yukimuro Sakekasu Ramen (雪むろ酒かすラーメン) — Joetsu's Own Ramen!
- Developed 2017 by Joetsu Aimenkai (上越愛麺会)
- **Trademarked** by Japanese Patent Office 2021
- Rules: Joetsu-produced sake lees, ingredients stored in snow rooms (yukimuro), topped with fermented foods + local vegetables/seafood
- Served at **19 establishments** across Joetsu City
- Bridges sake culture and ramen culture — genuine only-in-Joetsu creation

#### Other Local Specialties

- **Takenoko-jiru (たけのこ汁)**: Bamboo shoot + miso soup, Myoko staple
- **Mountain vegetables (山菜)**: Warabi, zenmai, wild bamboo shoots — seasonal spring/early summer
- **Red Yakisoba (赤やきそば)**: Noodles kneaded with Myoko highland vegetables
- **Noppe-jiru (のっぺい汁)**: Root vegetable stew, New Year staple
- **Nadachi Shokudo ramen**: Traditional ramen topped with kuruma-fu (車麩, round wheat gluten — Niigata specialty)
