## Design Rules (from project-memory/project_myokojoetsu_website.md)

- **Voice**: Third-person. The PLACE is the subject, not Sho. No "I" or "we"
- **No ComInc. branding**: No Sho profile, no "Cultural Translation", no booking forms, no pricing
- **Tone**: "hand-rolled soba from buckwheat harvested in October" — NOT "amazing food"
- **Sensory**: Include smells, sounds, textures ("the cedar-scented air of a 200-year-old warehouse")
- **No comparisons**: Never "better than Niseko". Quiet confidence

## Page-by-Page Content Brief

### SHOP (買 / kau)
**Editorial angle**: "Things worth carrying home."
**Design**: Each product = 1 editorial article page (story-first, buy button at bottom)
**Key content areas**:
- Kanzuri — snow-fermented chili paste, 3 years aging (flagship product)
- Sake — Ayumasamune (1875), Kiminoi, Chiyonohikari
- Setsuren-cha — lotus tea from Takada Castle moat
- Okina-ame, Sasa dango — traditional sweets
- Gangi covered arcades of Takada — living shopping culture
- Niigata koshihikari rice

**CTA**: STORES external link for each product (launching summer 2026)
**Phase 1**: Teaser/waitlist. **Phase 2**: Individual product article pages

### EAT (食 / taberu)
**Editorial angle**: "Food that rarely leaves the prefecture."
**Key content areas**:
- Hegi soba — Niigata's signature noodle
- Sake tasting — brewery visits
- Japan Sea seafood from Naoetsu port
- Local specialties: sasazushi, sasa dango
- Myoko highland vegetables
- Rice culture — Niigata is Japan's rice kingdom

**CTA**: Guide experience bookings (sake brewery tours, food walks)

### SEE (観 / miru)
**Editorial angle**: "A sacred mountain, a castle in bloom, and thirteen meters of snow."
**Key content areas**:
- Takada Castle — top 3 night sakura, summer lotus
- Mt. Myoko (2,454m) — sacred since 8th century
- Mt. Hiuchi (2,462m) — 100 Famous Mountains
- Naena Falls — 100 Famous Waterfalls
- Imori Pond — mirror of Myoko
- Gangi arcades of Takada
- Kasugayama Castle — Uesugi Kenshin's fortress
- Naoetsu port town, Gion Festival

**CTA**: Guided tour bookings

### STAY (泊 / tomaru)
**Editorial angle**: "Hot springs, old wood, and the sound of snow falling."

**IMPORTANT**: The kominka is in **central Takada castle town** (NOT rural/farmhouse).
Key distances from kominka:
- 5 min walk to Takada Castle Ruins Park
- 10 min walk to gangi pub street
- 25 min to ski mountains (Lotte Arai etc.)
- 25 min to Sea of Japan coastline/beach
- Muji-furnished interior (selling point)

**Key content areas**:
- **Kominka stays** — 130-year house in Takada castle town. Irori, tatami, beams. Urban-historic + mountain/ocean access
- **Akakura Onsen** — hot spring village on Myoko's slopes
- **Other Myoko onsen** — Tsubame Onsen, Seki Onsen (outdoor riverside bath)
- **Mountain lodges** — ski/hiker lodges
- **Lotte Arai Resort** — modern luxury option

**NOTE**: On myokojoetsu.com (third-person media), do NOT directly promote Sho's kominka.
Describe kominka stays as a *category*, not as Sho's specific listing. Keep the editorial distance.
cominc.co is where the personal pitch lives.

**CTA**: General "explore accommodations" — no direct Airbnb link on this site

### WINTER (冬 / fuyu — Dec–Mar)
**Editorial hook**: "The quietest season."
**Key content areas**:
- 13–16 meters of snow
- Ski resorts: Akakura, Madarao, Lotte Arai, etc.
- Onsen in snow
- Sake brewing season (new sake pressed)
- Winter food: nabe, mochi, preserved foods
- The silence of heavy snowfall

### SPRING (春 / haru — Apr–May)

**Key content areas**:

- Transition from white to green
- Mizubasho at Imori Pond

### SUMMER (夏 / natsu — Jun–Aug)
**Editorial hook**: "Above the clouds."
**Key content areas**:
- Mt. Myoko & Mt. Hiuchi hiking
- Lotus blooms on Takada Castle moat
- Summer festivals, gangi lanterns
- Beach access on Japan Sea coast
- Escape from city heat

### AUTUMN (秋 / aki — Sep–Nov)
**Editorial hook**: "The clearest skies of the year."
**Key content areas**:

- New-crop sake (hiyaoroshi)

- Autumn seafood
- Clear weather, Japan Sea views

## Decisions for Sho

1. **Domain**: Is `myokojoetsu.com` already connected to Cloudflare Pages? What's the live URL?
2. **Photos**: Do you have photos for each page? The gradient placeholders work for now, but real photos will transform the site.
3. **Kominka link**: Should the Stay page link directly to Airbnb, or to a cominc.co booking page?
4. **Shop page**: The landing page says "Launching summer 2026" — is the shop still coming? Should the page be a waitlist/teaser or have actual products?
5. **Guide bookings**: How should people book tours? Email form? Airbnb Experiences link? Future cominc.co form?
6. **Japanese version**: Should pages be bilingual (EN/JP) or separate language versions?
7. **SEO priority**: Which pages should we build first? Recommendation: Stay + Winter (highest commercial intent for ski season bookings)
